⚡ Electrolyte Imbalance

NCLEX Review

📌 Normal Values

Na⁺ 135–145 mEq/L
K⁺ 3.5–5.0 mEq/L
Ca²⁺ 8.5–10.5 mg/dL
🧂 Sodium (Na⁺)
Hyponatremia <135
  • Nausea, confusion
  • Seizure risk ⚠️
  • SIADH → Fluid restriction
Management
Hypertonic saline (severe)
Hypernatremia >145
  • Thirst, dehydration
  • Neuro changes
Management
Give fluids slowly (prevent cerebral edema)
❤️ Potassium (K⁺)
Hypokalemia <3.5
  • Muscle weakness
  • Arrhythmia
  • Flattened T wave
  • U waves on ECG ⚠️
Management
Oral/IV KCl
Hyperkalemia >5.0
  • Cardiac arrest risk ⚠️
  • Peaked T wave
  • Arrhythmia
Management
Calcium gluconate
Insulin + glucose
Dialysis (severe)
🔵 Calcium (Ca²⁺)
Hypocalcemia <8.5
  • Tetany, numbness
  • Chvostek sign
  • Trousseau sign
Management
Calcium IV
Hypercalcemia >10.5
  • Kidney stones
  • Constipation
  • Bone pain
  • Lethargy, confusion
Management
IV fluids + loop diuretics

⚠️ Critical Alerts

⚡ K⁺ ↑ = Cardiac arrest
⚠️ Na⁺ ↓ = Seizure risk!

🧠 Memory Tricks

① Low K = Low heart ❤️
② High K = Heart stop 🛑

🫁 ABG Made Easy

pH · PaCO₂ · HCO₃⁻

📌 Normal Values

pH 7.35–7.45
PaCO₂ 35–45 mmHg
HCO₃⁻ 22–26 mEq/L

🔥 Quick Diagnosis

Resp. Acidosis ↓pH, ↑CO₂
Resp. Alkalosis ↑pH, ↓CO₂
Met. Acidosis ↓pH, ↓HCO₃⁻
Met. Alkalosis ↑pH, ↑HCO₃⁻

⚡ Magic Trick

ROME RULE
Respiratory = Opposite direction
Metabolic = Equal / Same direction

🧠 Stepwise Interpretation

Step 1 — Check pH
↓ pH = Acidosis  |  ↑ pH = Alkalosis
Step 2 — Check PaCO₂ (Respiratory)
↑ CO₂ = Acidosis  |  ↓ CO₂ = Alkalosis
Step 3 — Check HCO₃⁻ (Metabolic)
↓ HCO₃⁻ = Acidosis  |  ↑ HCO₃⁻ = Alkalosis

🩺 Clinical Clues

COPD → Resp. Acidosis
Anxiety → Resp. Alkalosis
Diarrhea → Met. Acidosis
Vomiting → Met. Alkalosis

🧠 Memory Line: CO₂ = Acid   |   HCO₃⁻ = Base

⚠️ ALERT: pH < 7.2 or > 7.6 → EMERGENCY 🚨

🦋 Hyperthyroidism vs Hypothyroidism

NCLEX Review
🔴 Hyperthyroidism
High Metabolism State
TSH ↓  |  T3/T4 ↑
  • Weight loss despite ↑ appetite
  • Heat intolerance + sweating
  • Tachycardia (AFib risk), palpitations
  • Fine tremors, anxiety, irritability
  • Diarrhea
  • Graves' disease → Exophthalmos
⚠️ Thyroid Storm = High fever + severe tachycardia + delirium (EMERGENCY)
💊 Treatment
  • Methimazole / PTU
  • Beta blockers (Propranolol)
  • Radioactive iodine
  • Thyroidectomy
🔵 Hypothyroidism
Low Metabolism State
TSH ↑  |  T3/T4 ↓
  • Weight gain
  • Cold intolerance
  • Bradycardia
  • Constipation
  • Dry skin, hair loss
  • Puffy face (Myxedema)
⚠️ Myxedema Coma = Hypothermia + bradycardia + hypotension (EMERGENCY)
💊 Treatment
  • Levothyroxine (empty stomach, morning)
  • Lifelong therapy
  • Start low dose in elderly/cardiac patients

⚡ Super Fast Memory Trick

🔥 Hyper = Hot · Fast · Losing weight
❄️ Hypo = Cold · Slow · Gaining weight
